AdaJEPA — online gradient TTA

Takes gradient steps on the world model's own prediction loss over a buffer of transitions the controller has actually executed. The correction is an optimizer trajectory: it accumulates across replans and is never recomputed from the frozen weights. Rank-2 LoRA on the predictor's last block, which the predecessor found sufficient to match a dense update.
